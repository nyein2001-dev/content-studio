
# 🥇 1. MONEY & ECONOMICS

## Channel/Niche Name

### **Money, Markets & Economics**

I would eventually choose a short consumer-facing brand name rather than literally calling the channel "Personal Finance."

The editorial promise:

> **"Understand the money systems that control everyday life."**

This is broader and more defensible than a generic "finance tips" channel.

---

## Core Topic

Explain:

* money
* prices
* inflation
* wages
* debt
* interest
* investing concepts
* taxes
* housing economics
* consumer economics
* banking
* markets
* business economics
* economic incentives
* wealth-building principles

**Not** day-trading signals or "buy this stock."

That distinction is important.

---

## Target Audience

Approximately 18–45:

* young professionals
* students
* entrepreneurs
* people trying to understand money
* investors
* financially curious viewers
* people confused by economic news

Global audience, with localization later.

---

## Why Humans Actually Care

Money affects almost every major life decision.

People constantly ask:

* Why is everything getting more expensive?
* Why can't I afford a house?
* Why does debt grow so quickly?
* Why do banks make money?
* Why does inflation happen?
* Why does compound interest matter?
* Why do salaries differ?
* Why are mortgages so expensive?
* Why do markets crash?
* Why does money lose purchasing power?

These are **recurring human problems**, not entertainment trends.

---

## Why It Has Long-Term Demand

Extremely strong.

Money systems change, but the underlying questions don't.

You can continuously generate:

* new economic data
* new inflation numbers
* new housing data
* new consumer behavior
* new financial products
* historical comparisons
* country comparisons
* economic explanations
* "what if" simulations

A video about compound interest can work today and five years from now.

A video about a specific TikTok trend may not.

**10/10**

Extremely strong.

## Shorts/TikTok/Reels Potential

**10/10**

* "Wait until you see the final number"

* simulations
* counters
* before/after

## Global Audience

**10/10**

Money exists everywhere.

The trick is to separate:

**Universal economics**

from

**country-specific financial advice.**

For global content:

> "Why inflation makes your money weaker"

is better than:

> "How Thailand's tax deduction works."

## Main Risks

* financial misinformation
* outdated figures
* jurisdiction-specific advice
* investment advice liability
* overpromising
* sensationalism
* lower trust if AI voice sounds generic

The playbook explicitly requires numbers to be computed from stated inputs or cited from named sources and requires assumptions/disclaimers where relevant, particularly finance and health. 

---

## Why I Rank It #1

Because it has the best combination of:

**huge demand + high advertiser value + endless data + visual explainability + global relevance.**

It is not simply a "finance channel."

It can become a **visual economics media brand**.
